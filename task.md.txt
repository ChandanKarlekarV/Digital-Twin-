## 2. HARDWARE & MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION KERNEL

The application must run an internal high-speed synthetic Multiphase Flow Meter (MPFM) & Coriolis sensor engine producing data at 10 Hz (100ms ticks), mathematically verified against international petroleum standards:

### A. Synthetic Coriolis Density & Resonant Frequency Model
Oscillating tube frequency:
$$f = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m_{\text{tube}} + \rho \cdot V_{\text{tube}}}}$$
Transmitter computation of uncompensated density:
$$\rho_{\text{obs}}(T) = C_1 \cdot \tau^2 - C_2 + K_T (T - T_0)$$
Where:
- $\tau = 1/f$ (Oscillation period in microseconds, typically $1100 - 1500\,\mu\text{s}$)
- $C_1 = 4.2875 \times 10^{-4}$, $C_2 = 0.5218$, $K_T = -0.0125\,\text{kg/m}^3/^\circ\text{C}$, $T_0 = 20.0^\circ\text{C}$.

### B. Standard Density & API Gravity (ASTM D1250 / API MPMS Ch. 11.1)
Base water density at $60^\circ\text{F}$ ($15.56^\circ\text{C}$) is $\rho_{\text{water, 60}} = 999.016\,\text{kg/m}^3$.
$$\text{SG}_{60/60} = \frac{\rho_{\text{oil, 60}}}{\rho_{\text{water, 60}}}$$
$$^\circ\text{API} = \frac{141.5}{\text{SG}_{60/60}} - 131.5 \iff \text{SG}_{60/60} = \frac{141.5}{^\circ\text{API} + 131.5}$$

### C. Temperature & Pressure Volume Correction Factors (VCF)
$$\rho_{\text{base}} = \frac{\rho_{\text{obs}}}{\text{CTL} \times \text{CPL}}$$
$$\text{CTL} = \exp\left[-\alpha_T \Delta T (1 + 0.8 \alpha_T \Delta T)\right]$$
Where:
$$\alpha_T = \frac{K_0}{\rho_{\text{base}}^2} + \frac{K_1}{\rho_{\text{base}}}$$
($K_0 = 341.0957$, $K_1 = 0.0$ for KG-D6 light condensate/crude oil, $\Delta T = T_{\text{obs}} - 15.56^\circ\text{C}$).
$$\text{CPL} = \frac{1}{1 - F \cdot (P_{\text{obs}} - P_e)}$$
Where $F$ is isothermal fluid compressibility ($F \approx 1.25 \times 10^{-5}\,\text{bar}^{-1}$) and $P_e$ is equilibrium vapor pressure.

### D. Multiphase Cut & Net Oil Deconvolution
$$\rho_{\text{mix}} = \text{WC} \cdot \rho_{\text{water}} + (1 - \text{WC}) \cdot \rho_{\text{oil}}$$
$$\text{WC} = \frac{\rho_{\text{mix}} - \rho_{\text{oil}}}{\rho_{\text{water}} - \rho_{\text{oil}}}$$
$$\dot{m}_{\text{net\_oil}} = \dot{m}_{\text{total}} \times \left[1 - \left(\text{WC} \cdot \frac{\rho_{\text{water}}}{\rho_{\text{mix}}}\right)\right]$$
$$\text{Net BPD} = \frac{\dot{m}_{\text{net\_oil}}\ (\text{kg/day})}{\rho_{\text{base}} \times 0.1589873}$$

### E. Signal Denoising (Real-Time 1D Kalman Filter)
For noisy and corrupted raw telemetry, the engine executes a continuous state estimator:
$$x_k^- = A x_{k-1} + B u_k, \quad P_k^- = A P_{k-1} A^T + Q$$
$$K_k = P_k^- H^T (H P_k^- H^T + R)^{-1}$$
$$x_k = x_k^- + K_k (z_k - H x_k^-), \quad P_k = (I - K_k H) P_k^-$$
Detecting sensor drift and zero-clipping bad packets instantly.
